Dear App Review team,

All requested information is below and also saved in the Notes field of App Review Information. A screen recording is attached to version 1.0.

1. SCREEN RECORDING
Attached. Recorded on a physical iPhone 13 with VoiceOver active - the speech you hear is the app being operated the way its users operate it. It begins with launching the app and covers the typical flow: the "What's new" list, playing an episode with Magic Tap to pause and resume, podcasts and articles by category, an article, reader comments, search, the live radio stream, the schedule, and the live-show contact feature (text and voice message).
There is no registration, login, account deletion, in-app purchase or paid content, so those flows do not appear - they do not exist in the app.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Official client for Tyflopodcast, Tyfloswiat and Tyfloradio - Polish media services for blind and partially sighted people, run by the Institute for Regional Development Foundation, a non-profit. The three services live on separate websites and navigating them with a screen reader is slow and error-prone; this app brings them into one VoiceOver-first interface.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
No credentials are needed, because none exist. Every feature works immediately after install, so no demo account or sample files are required.
Tabs: Nowosci (What's new), Podcasty, Artykuly (Articles), Szukaj (Search), Tyfloradio (radio, schedule, contact). With VoiceOver on, Magic Tap (two-finger double tap) pauses and resumes playback.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Our own servers only: tyflopodcast.net and tyfloswiat.pl (public WordPress REST API), the Tyfloradio HLS stream, and kontakt.tyflopodcast.net for the contact form. No third-party data provider, authentication service, payment processor, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or AI service. Push notifications are disabled here: the UI is inactive in the Release build and no device token is transmitted.

5. TIME-DEPENDENT FEATURE - LIKELY WHY THE FIRST REVIEW LOOKED INCOMPLETE
The contact form is visible ONLY while an interactive show is on air. The app asks our server (parameter ac=current) and reveals the section when a show is live; otherwise it is hidden. This is intended behaviour, not a bug. If the app was reviewed outside broadcast hours, this key feature was invisible. The attached recording was made deliberately DURING a live broadcast, so the whole flow is visible.
We do not fake a "show is live" state for review: the same flag serves our Android app, Windows app and website, so switching it on would invite real listeners to contact a show that is not on air. To see it live, contact us via our Support URL and we will give you the date and time of the next broadcast.
Microphone use is limited to recording a voice message for the show, always started by the user, never in the background.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Identical features and behaviour in all territories. Interface and content are in Polish because the services are Polish - intentional, not a missing localisation. Only the contact section changes, and it changes over TIME (point 5), never by location.

7. RIGHTS TO THE CONTENT

This app is published by the person who runs the editorial side of these services. Tyflopodcast recordings are also released under Creative Commons BY 3.0 PL.

8. USER CONTENT - READ ONLY
The app displays reader comments on episodes, strictly read-only: there is no field or button anywhere to write, submit or upload a comment. Comments are moderated by our team before publication and are public on the website. Should we add posting later, we will implement reporting and blocking mechanisms and update our privacy answers first.
